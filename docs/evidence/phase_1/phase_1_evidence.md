# Phase 1 \- Evidence \- References used to inform the V0.0 material

## Version 0.0

---

### Introductory note

This is a list of books, articles, and other sources that inspired version 0.0 of the tasks and responsibilities of theorists in psychology. It is a cumulative body of literature rather than a set of references assigned to individual tasks: the eight responsibilities in version 0.0 were formulated by drawing on this literature as a whole. 

Version 0.0 of the tasks and responsibilities was revised after the consensus review. The current version, version 0.1, also reflects the feedback of the ECTO experts and the sources and perspectives they brought in. This list therefore documents where the responsibilities started, not everything that informs their present wording.
