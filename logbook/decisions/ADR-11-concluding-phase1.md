# ADR-0011: Concluding Phase 1 of ECTO

- Date: 2026-07-03
- Status: Accepted
- Decision owner: Project lead
- Related: 

## Context
Consensus was reached on all 8 items of Phase 1 and several new items were suggested to be added to the original list.

## Decision
No new items will be added. Suggested new items will be incorporated into the 8 existing items or written as "important but delegable tasks." Experts will be offered the opportunity to disagree with this decision. If they do, Phase 1 Round 2 will still be initiated with the new items. 

## Rationale
Though new items were suggested for addition to the list, other experts said the list was already quite long. Also, I thought that all could be fitted in the original list of 8 items or should be the sole or core responsibility of theorists. 

## Consequences
Phase 1 will be concluded, and development of Phase 2 will start early.
