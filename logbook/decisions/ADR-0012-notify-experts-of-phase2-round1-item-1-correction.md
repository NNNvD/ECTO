# ADR-0012: Notify experts of the Phase 2 Round 1 Item 1 correction

- Date: 2026-10-22
- Status: Accepted
- Decision owner: Project lead
- Related: [Item 1 wording correction incident](../incidents/2026-10-22-phase2-round1-item-1-wording-correction.md)

## Context
Five experts completed the Phase 2 Round 1 survey before the wording of Item 1 was corrected.

## Decision
Notify each affected expert individually by email and offer them the opportunity to revise their evaluation before the 8 November 2026 deadline. Include the corrected version of Item 1 and the expert's own original response in their email.

## Rationale
Seeing the corrected wording alongside their original response allows each expert to decide whether to revise their rating or explanation.

## Consequences
The affected experts were notified individually. Original evaluations and any revisions are retained separately in the restricted audit trail. The analysis decisions are recorded in [ADR-0013](ADR-0013-phase2-round1-response-handling.md).

## Alternatives considered
A possible alternative was to correct the wording for future respondents without inviting revisions from the experts who had already completed the survey. This would have left those experts without an opportunity to evaluate the corrected item.
