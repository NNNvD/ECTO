# 2026-10-22 — Incident — Phase 2 Round 1 Item 1 wording correction

## Summary
Item 1, “Identify and formulate explananda,” in the Phase 2 Round 1 survey contained incorrect wording. Five experts submitted responses before the wording was corrected. This public note records the process issue without including identities or response-level information.

## Timeline
- The survey was distributed with incorrect wording for Item 1.
- Five responses were submitted before the issue was corrected.
- The wording of Item 1 was corrected in the survey.
- Individual follow-up with affected respondents is planned; completion is pending.

## Impact
The five pre-correction evaluations of Item 1 may reflect the incorrect wording rather than the intended item. The issue was limited to Item 1; no other affected items have been identified. No individual responses or identifying information are included in this note.

## Root cause
A mismatch between the intended and deployed wording of Item 1. The cause of the mismatch has not yet been established.

## Resolution
The survey wording for Item 1 has been corrected.

- Preserve original evaluations and record any revisions separately.
